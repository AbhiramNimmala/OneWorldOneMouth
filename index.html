Why it matters

Hunger in Georgia is closer than it looks

From Atlanta suburbs to rural counties, food insecurity reaches families who are working, studying, and retiring right next door.

Overall food insecurity1.4M

Georgians lived in food-insecure households between 2020 and 2022, about 13.2% of the state.

Child hunger555,208

Georgia children live in households without enough food, the fourth highest rate in the nation.

Seniors13%

of older Georgians face food insecurity.

Working adults9.6%

of employed adults in Georgia experience food insecurity.

Food deserts

Nearly 2 million residents, including about 500,000 children, live without easy access to fresh, affordable food.

School meals

As of October 2024, 67.5% of Georgia public school students qualified for free or reduced-price lunch.

SNAP gaps

In 2018, 16% of eligible Georgians did not receive SNAP benefits.

Summer meals

Georgia declined the federal Summer EBT program in 2024, leaving about $138 million in food aid for over 1.1 million children unclaimed.

The global picture: roughly 1 in 11 people worldwide face hunger, and 144 million children under five are stunted by malnutrition. Our name is a reminder that it is one world, and every mouth counts.

Partners

Who we work with

Meals by Grace

A Fill Ministries, Inc. initiative · Cumming, GA · since 2011

17,000+

volunteers support Meals by Grace every year.

Stephen and Suellen Daniels founded Meals by Grace after their own experience with financial hardship, to fight food insecurity across Forsyth and Dawson counties.

School social workers identify families in need, and Meals by Grace supports them with hot meals delivered home, a client-choice pantry, and mobile pantries. Its aquaponic farm grows fresh produce and teaches nutrition so families can build self-sufficiency.

  • Home-delivered meals
  • Client-choice pantry
  • Mobile pantries
  • Aquaponic farm
War Eagle Food Pantry

South Forsyth High School

Runs regular food drives and mobilizes student volunteers. Its pantry has become a model for other schools in the district.

Danes Care

Denmark High School

One of the district's newer schools, coordinating monthly food collection and partnering with local businesses to stretch every drive.

Longhorns Helping Hands

Lambert High School

Student council runs year-round collection and works with school counselors to find and support families in need.

Wolverines Care

West Forsyth High School

A student-led program covering both large-scale collection and distribution, so support reaches the families who need it most.
